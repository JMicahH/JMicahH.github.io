Lia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What Lia accesses

  • Gmail (read): to find tasks, deadlines and messages that need your attention.
  • Gmail (drafts): to create draft replies for you to review. Lia does not send email from your account.
  • Google Calendar (read): to plan your day around your events.
  • A separate "Lia" calendar: Lia creates and edits time blocks only on a calendar it creates. It never changes your other calendars.

How your data is used

  • Data is used only to provide Lia's features to you.
  • Email and calendar content is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to sort, summarize and draft. It is not used to train AI models.
  • Tasks, notes and settings are stored in an encrypted database (Supabase). Google sign-in tokens are encrypted.
  • Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or read by people except when you ask for help or to fix a security issue.

Google API data

Lia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect Lia anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Email micah.haid@gmail.com to have all your data deleted.
